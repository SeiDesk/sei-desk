## Preserve the accepted headless contract

[Stage 3A acceptance](STAGE-3A-ACCEPTANCE-REPORT.md), [launch semantics](STAGE-3A-LAUNCH-SERVICE.md), [Stage 3B acceptance](STAGE-3B-ACCEPTANCE-REPORT.md)/[independent review](STAGE-3B-PATCH-REVIEW.md)/[consumer contract](STAGE-3B-CONSUMER-ARCHITECTURE.md) remain the source of truth:

- One headless owner; one occupied refresh lane and one launch lane, at most two service workers. Redundant work is **rejected Busy**, not coalesced. Completion and cancellation do not prematurely free a blocked lane.
- Successful refresh replaces generation and **clears selection even for a retained identity**. Failed/cancelled refresh retains usable snapshot/selection. Do not implement J's optional identity-retention idea over this accepted contract. Keyboard focus may remain on the list separately, but Launch remains disabled until explicit current-snapshot selection.
- Explicit launch captures the selected request and generation. New selection cannot retarget it. A replacement before dispatch rejects stale selection; acceptance after dispatch must still be reported against the original ID even if refresh changed the catalog.
- Checked operation IDs do not wrap; failure can mean unknown external acceptance. Never auto-retry an uncertain launch. Success/PID means creation accepted, not application readiness or foreground activation.
- Cancellation is cooperative; filesystem/COM/CreateProcess may block. Do not kill a worker or claim cancellation terminates an accepted app. `wait`, `drain_ready` (which joins), `shutdown`/Drop and worker joins must remain off the GUI thread. The unwind completion guard does not guarantee recovery from abort, endless destructors or uninterruptible OS calls.
- Stage 3A target/argument/cwd/source restrictions, structured errors and no automatic elevation/shell fallback remain unchanged. NativeService does not keep a Child for future app termination. Any later fixture backend retains only its **owned test child** for explicit cleanup.

These are source/historical headless guarantees, not proof of a future UI-to-owner adapter. Its bounded command/wakeup/acknowledgement protocol needs separately approved design and native UI evidence. No adapter, bridge or service change is implemented here. Twenty-three root/crate hashes match the preserved lifecycle-fix tested tree; that is an integrity check, not a fresh execution.

**A — first future experiment:** use fixed names/IDs and fake catalog service outcomes with deterministic gates. Real widgets/provider/native UIA/manual interactions are required; fake discovery/backends are labelled as such. Launch is an intent-only recorder, explicitly presented as a qualification dry run; it neither creates a process nor claims service launch success. Selection must create **zero intents and zero processes**. This can test native Invoke routing once the provider is supported, not actual application launch semantics. Do not enumerate or launch installed applications.

**B — optional later separate approval:** only after A's applicable entry checks pass and owner explicitly approves exact harmless fixture hash/path/argv/cwd, real process creation, ownership/cleanup and time budget. B uses no arbitrary discovered application. Passing B is not UI-entry approval. A's dry-run result cannot be counted as B.

**Release coverage:** A_RELEASE cases and actual product reruns remain mandatory before a separately authorized product could ship. Mixed-scale and negative-coordinate displays are explicitly required; an unavailable mandatory configuration blocks that scope. No profile or setting change is implicit. A human/host availability failure stops required runtime testing rather than replacing it with synthetic input.

Proposed fixed fixtures: empty, one entry, three selection entries, 40 rows for scrolling, and a bounded 200-entry metadata/resource set. These are test-data recommendations, not new product limits. Proposed outer lifetime is ten minutes **per owned run**, subject to O05 approval; total runs/effort budget remains unapproved. Finite diagnostic sampling is test-client activity, not permission for app polling.

Before execution, O03/O05 must fill versioned numeric limits: queue capacity C, metadata units/limit N, allowed retained status/diagnostic sizes, CPU and memory thresholds, max/p95 response latency, geometry rounding tolerance in physical pixels, repeat-session/handle drift tolerance, total runs and observation budget. No manufactured performance guarantee or arbitrary passing threshold is supplied. Missing limits block resource/boundary acceptance. Native events and operation gates establish order; deadlines are hang safeguards only, never a recovery mechanism or proof of correctness.
